Yes. PdfCraft is free and open source, under the MIT or Apache-2.0 license (your choice). It needs no account.

## What about my documents?

Documents never leave your machine. PdfCraft has no telemetry and no cloud processing, and it works offline. The home screen says: "PdfCraft works offline. No telemetry, no account, and no cloud processing unless you add a provider."

## Where is the code?

On [GitHub](https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft). PdfCraft is part of the Crafting Apps from the ArtCraft team, which are free, open-source creative tools. See [Install PdfCraft](../getting-started/install.md) to download it.
