Shortcuts on this page use Ctrl. On macOS, use Cmd instead of Ctrl.

## Open your first PDF

1. Start PdfCraft. With no document open it shows the **Welcome to PdfCraft** home screen.
2. Click **Open file**, or drop a PDF anywhere on the window. You can also open the menu and choose **File**, then **Open…** (`Ctrl+O`).
3. Files you open appear under **Recent** on the home screen.

Next: [Open and read a PDF](../guides/open-and-read.md).

PdfCraft shows tools in the **All tools** panel. Press `Ctrl+K` to search every tool and command by name.
